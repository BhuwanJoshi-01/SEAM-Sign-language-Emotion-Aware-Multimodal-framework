what the system does and does not claim
No human preference data exists. The avatar-quality study (M7) has stimuli and a measurement harness but zero raters. Nothing on any of these pages is a result about how good the avatar looks. Any claim to that effect would be fabricated.
Affect and gloss are withheld from the live demo on purpose. Affect is withheld because M4 measured 0.497 balanced accuracy against a 0.5 reference. Gloss recognition is withheld because M5a's WER (0.916) equals its most-frequent baseline. A number that carries no information is worse than no number, so both are withheld rather than shown empty.

Other measured results

  • M1 confound audit — refuted. No marker-induced negative FER bias is detectable across 2,565 clips and 3 non-signer models: −0.008…+0.002 against an MDE of 0.003–0.008. A powered null, not an absence of measurement.
  • M4 factorisation — refuted. Worst-fold cross-prediction AUC 0.7276 (heuristic y_L) and 0.7031 (human y_L) against a ≤0.60 target, with the signer positive control at 0.973 so the instrument demonstrably works. The core contribution as originally framed was withdrawn.
  • M3 label quality. Human SignStream non-manual annotations for all 200 utterances; Cohen's kappa 0.639 (negation) and 0.734 (rhetorical question) usable, 0.028 and 0.038 at chance for the other two categories.
  • M2 efficiency. 73.2 ms p95 perception, 186 MB peak for six models resident at once against a 2,500 MB ceiling, 20.4 FPS sustained (repeats 19.7–20.4, so K6 is met at the median and missed at the low end).
  • M5a gloss recognition — negative. WER 0.916 against a most-frequent baseline of 0.916 and a shuffled-label control of 0.911.

Every number above traces to a run ID in paper/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md, and a test fails if one does not. Full detail in PROJECT_BREAKDOWN.md.
