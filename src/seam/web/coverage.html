Other measured results

M1 confound audit — refuted on isolated signs. No brow- or mouth-marker-induced negative FER bias is detectable across 2,565 WLASL clips and 3 non-signer models: −0.008…+0.002 against an MDE of 0.003–0.008. A powered null, not an absence of measurement. It says nothing about head shake, for which there is no working instrument (one matched pair after the yaw fix).

The same question on continuous signing — not supported. 200 utterances, human frame-level markers, 0.5 s windows inside the signing span, a decision rule fixed before the first run. No marker meets it. Brow furrow leans the other way in all three models; brow raise and rhetorical questions lean positive in two and do not survive correction. Yes/no and wh-questions have too few clips to estimate, and these are small FER models, so this is not evidence that the confound does not exist.

M4 factorisation — refuted. Worst-fold cross-prediction AUC 0.694 (heuristic y_L) and 0.726 (human y_L) against a ≤0.60 target, with the signer positive control at 0.98 so the instrument demonstrably works. The gate fails in every one of three seeds and five ablation variants. The core contribution as originally framed was withdrawn.

M3 label quality. Human SignStream non-manual annotations for all 200 utterances; Cohen's kappa 0.639 (negation) and 0.734 (rhetorical question) usable, 0.028 and 0.038 at chance for the other two categories. The visual markers were checked against human frame labels, leave one signer out: brow raise is validated (within-clip AUC 0.82–0.88), brow furrow is not (0.60), and head shake and head nod are unreadable even by a fitted detector. An earlier negation ↔ head-shake association (r = 0.554) was produced by a rotation bug and is withdrawn.

M2 efficiency. 73.2 ms p95 perception, 186 MB peak for six models resident at once against a 2,500 MB ceiling, 20.4 FPS sustained (repeats 19.7–20.4, so K6 is met at the median and missed at the low end).

M5a gloss recognition — gate not met. WER 0.920 against a most-frequent baseline of 0.920, signer-disjoint, on 1,736 tokens over 546 glosses. The first run of this experiment (0.916 against 0.916) was not a measurement: its model predicted a single gloss, and its token frames were misaligned on the 138 clips recorded at 24 fps. Both are corrected; the verdict is the same and is now from an instrument that passes a positive control.

Frame alignment. ASLLRP frame indices are on a 30 fps timeline while 138 of the 200 clips are 24 fps. Checked against an independent signal — annotated blinks against the eye-blink blendshape — the corrected mapping scores AUC 0.710 on those clips where frame-for-frame scores 0.554.

Every number above traces to a run ID in paper/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md, and a test fails if one does not. Full detail in PROJECT_BREAKDOWN.md.
