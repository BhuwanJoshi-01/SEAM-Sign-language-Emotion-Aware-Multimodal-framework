What this is — and is not

No human preference data exists. The avatar-quality study (M7) has stimuli and a measurement harness but zero raters. Nothing on this page is a result about how good the avatar looks. Any claim to that effect would be fabricated.
Pose comes from SMPLer-X, a third-party regressor trained on mocap. It knows nothing about sign language and will render plausible signing whatever the hands say. Its output must not be cited as evidence about linguistic content.
The numbers below are pipeline measurements — is the front end running, does it detect the signer, did it collapse, is the body upright. They are deliberately not quality scores. A confidently wrong pipeline produces a similar table.
